# In your own words

Tell the user, in plain language and your own words:

1. **The goal.** What they want to end up with, as an outcome, not a list of steps.
2. **The problem.** Why they need it. What's wrong or missing today.
3. **The smallest change that would do it**, if the task changes code or systems.
4. **What you're assuming.** Anything you filled in that they didn't say. Mark each one so it's easy to correct.

Keep it to a short paragraph plus the assumptions. Don't repeat their words back. Rephrasing is what exposes a misunderstanding.

If the evidence (the code, the docs, a quick check) shows the request won't reach the goal, say so and show why before going further.

Then continue with the task. Don't wait for confirmation unless an assumption, if wrong, would waste real work. In that case, ask about it in the same message.
